What it shows

For a full URL: scheme, host, path, fragment and every query parameter, with repeated keys collected into lists. For encoded text: each decoding step, so double-encoded values such as %2520 are visible.

Special URLs are recognised and passed on: OAuth authorize and callback URLs, SAML redirects and DNS-over-HTTPS queries.

Questions

Why does + turn into a space?

In form-encoded query strings + means space. Decoder follows that rule; a literal plus sign is encoded as %2B.
